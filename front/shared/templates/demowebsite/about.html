About AIKON-demo

AIKON-demo is a platform to run computer vision experiments on user-defined datasets. It is tailored to historical document analysis and offers state of the art algorithms for region extraction (image, diagram or text detection), similarity retrieval, watermark detection and image clustering.

Once you have selected the experiment to run, AIKON-demo has a 3-step workflow:

  1. Pick a dataset, or upload a new one
  2. Configure the experiment (which algorithm and parameters)
  3. Run the experiment and visualize results

AIKON-demo is developped cojointly with the AIKON platform, which offers research projects a collaborative environment for historical corpora management, computer vision and document analysis.
